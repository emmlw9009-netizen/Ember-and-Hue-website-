Ten house styles

Every frame tells the same story beat, 1816, the year without a summer, so you compare directions, not subjects.

Each style is a complete system: illustration, color, type, subtitles and chapter cards. The one you pick becomes the look of every long-form video.

Reply with a number, or mix two, like “2 with the type from 1”.
