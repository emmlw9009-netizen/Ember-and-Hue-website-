Ledger & Tack

LEDGER
The explainer. Tall, glasses, red tie, hair parted like a filing tab. Patient, precise, slowly cracking. The straight man.
TACK
The question guy. Half Ledger's height, cap two sizes too big, permanent blush. Asks dumb questions with their own logic.
HOW THE DYNAMIC WORKS

Ledger explains, Tack interrupts. Every dumb question sets up the next fact, so the jokes move the story forward. Ledger's patience drops a notch each time (rule of three), and once a video Tack is accidentally right. Tack gets the last line.
